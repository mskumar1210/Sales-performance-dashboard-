Key takeaways (full dataset)

  • Order lines discounted 21–40% average a −15.3% margin and lines above 40% a −77.4% margin; together they lost $135K.
  • Central earns a 7.9% margin versus 14.9% in West, with average discounts of 24% versus 11%.
  • Tables (−$17.7K), Bookcases (−$3.5K) and Supplies (−$1.2K) lose money overall.
  • Q4 brings 38% of annual sales; sales grew 29.5% in 2017 and 20.4% in 2018.
